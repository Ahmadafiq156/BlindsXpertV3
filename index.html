ABOUT BLINDSXPERT

Made for the way you use your space

Since 2005, BlindsXpert has supplied customised and ready-made window covering products. The official site describes an environmental focus and ongoing development of blinds technology. Its stated customer groups include Malaysian and international schools, IPTA/IPTS, government, private companies and individuals. Consultation and site measurement are available in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, with material selection at a customer’s home or office.
