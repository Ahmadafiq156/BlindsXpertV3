Products (Curtains, Blinds)

What types of blinds are available?

The catalogue lists roller, zebra, Venetian, Roman, vertical, panel, dream and honeycomb blinds, along with outdoor blinds and motorized solutions. Explore the Products page and the relevant product details.

Does BlindsXpert offer curtains and curtain hardware?

The current catalogue lists curtain rails and curtain rods. Please contact our team to confirm curtain fabric or curtain supply availability.

What is the difference between roller blinds and zebra blinds?

Roller blinds use a roll-up design; zebra blinds use alternating sheer and opaque fabric bands. Compare the roller blind and zebra blind details.

Are different colours and materials available?

The catalogue lists product-specific options, including roller blackout, sunscreen and translucent fabrics, and Venetian Aluminium, Timber, PVC and Lantex ranges. View the product details and confirm current colours and material availability with our team.

Can blinds be customized for different window sizes?

BlindsXpert describes customised and ready-made window coverings on its About page. Contact our team to discuss your window dimensions and confirm suitable product and customisation options.

Are motorized blinds available?

Motorized Solutions are listed in our catalogue, with product demonstrations and Somfy and Dooya ranges. Contact our team to confirm the options for your project.

Services (Measurement, Installation, Customization)

How do I arrange a site measurement?

Contact BlindsXpert to arrange site measurement. The Services page lists site measurement in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor; confirm appointment arrangements directly with our team.

Does BlindsXpert provide installation services?

The Services page includes Installation & Delivery as the final step in the customer journey. Contact our team to confirm installation arrangements for your project.

Can I request customized blinds?

Arrange a consultation with our team to discuss a customised blind requirement. The customer journey includes site measurement and product selection before quotation; confirm the suitable options with BlindsXpert.

How can I obtain a quotation?

Request a quotation through the quotation form, which opens an email draft with your project details, or contact our team by phone, WhatsApp or email.

How long does installation usually take?

Please contact our team to confirm the installation schedule for your project.
