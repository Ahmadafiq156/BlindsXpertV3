ABOUT BLINDSXPERT

Window covering solutions since 2005

BlindsXpert supplies customised and ready-made products for homes, schools, government, private companies and individuals.

OUR COMPANY

BlindsXpert Malaysia

Since its establishment in 2005, BlindsXpert has supplied customised and ready-made window covering products. The company provides installation and site measurement in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor.

Customers can arrange a consultation to discuss product selection and measurement for their home or office. The official site identifies Malaysian and international schools, IPTA/IPTS, government, private companies and individuals among its customer groups. It also says that if a customer is dissatisfied with a blind’s style, colour or quality, BlindsXpert will work with them to make it right.
