## Uncertain source information and corrections

- Zebra top label in BX 401BL prints **SP 402** on one line and **BL CREAM** on the next. The literal code/label are preserved as BX 402 / BL CREAM. Confirm whether BL is intended as part of the code before normalising it to BX 402BL. The actual cream sample is visible and sampled.
- The BX 201Z cream sample prints SP 207Z; the earlier SP 201Z transcription was corrected. All other original code characters and names remain as printed.
- Roller white is **RITZ 3001**, black in BX 5406 is **BX 5406R-9**, and BX 5406R-12 prints **STONE**. Earlier transcriptions were corrected after examining portrait-oriented source copies.
- SP 210 is classified Translucent by its folder, despite 5% openness on its sheet. The new source-based mapping supersedes the previous requested Sunscreen placement. Printed BRONE BLACK and GREEN / WHITE labels are retained.
- Carmen, Econo and Urban Stripe have no verified variant and remain selectable under classification pending. No Blackout/Sunscreen/Translucent assignment is guessed.
- Aurora colour codes have no printed names; no names were invented. Two Econo GREEN entries are distinct codes (EC 4013 and EC 4061). Dream code groups lack supplier collection names; code group labels are explicit, derived from the separate sheets.
- Zebra BX 601BL, BX 701BL and BX 801BL weight values print 156/m², 159/m² and 159/m² without a mass unit. They are retained literally and flagged, not expanded to g/m².
- Sunset composition prints 26% Polyester, 24% Cotton (total 50%); the remainder is missing. Urban Stripe strength/elongation units are unusual, and Carmen Transmittance lists an instrument without a numerical result. These are preserved literally and flagged.
- Dream sheets print Fabric Thikness **40cm±1cm**, Light Fastness percentages, and weight **75g±5g/m**. These are readable but unusual; they are preserved verbatim with a supplier-confirmation notice. No guessed unit corrections.
- SP 200 lists 3.0m width but no weight/thickness for that width. No missing values were extrapolated. Original JLS/IOS standard spellings remain as printed.
- No colour sample was unreadable or unlocatable. All 154 swatches were sampled from identified fabric areas. HEX values are approximate; no supplier photograph or specification image is displayed in the configurators.
